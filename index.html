Kassandra, Halkidiki

The peninsula combines lush forests that reach the sea, offering unique landscapes for hiking, cycling, or simply enjoying the tranquility of nature. Kassandra boasts some of Greece's most beautiful Blue Flag beaches with golden sands, clear, crystal-clear waters, and ideal conditions for relaxation. Beaches like Sani, Kallithea, and Polychrono attract visitors from around the world. The shallow beaches, organized facilities, and safety make Kassandra an ideal destination for families with children.

Kassandra is a place where tradition meets luxury, and natural beauty complements modern amenities. Whether you're seeking relaxation, adventure, or entertainment, Kassandra offers the perfect setting for your ideal vacation!
Polychrono, one of the most popular destinations in Halkidiki, offers excellent options for peaceful and unforgettable vacations. Located on the eastern side of the first peninsula (Kassandra), it combines clean beaches, a picturesque atmosphere, and good tourist infrastructure.
